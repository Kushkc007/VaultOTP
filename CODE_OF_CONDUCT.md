# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in VaultOTP to be harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive review.
- Respect privacy: **never** post real TOTP secrets, personal account data or credentials — yours or anyone else's.
- Keep discussion on topic and technical.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or sexualised attention.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately introducing vulnerabilities, backdoors or telemetry into a contribution.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests or discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject comments, commits and contributions that violate this document, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

Report issues privately to the maintainers listed in `package.json`. Reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.
